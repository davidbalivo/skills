# Spanish Artifacts

## Scope

Use this skill whenever the requested output is in Spanish, including:

- Translating existing content from English into Spanish.
- Creating a new artifact directly in Spanish.
- Editing, rewriting, or updating an existing Spanish-language artifact.

Artifacts include Jira tickets, Confluence or Notion pages, presentations, HTML pages, documents,
reports, emails, and similar deliverables. Follow the requested format, structure, audience, and
purpose of the target artifact.

## English Terminology

Keep terms in English when Spanish-speaking professionals commonly use the English form or when
translating them would sound unnatural, lose precision, or make them harder to recognize. Preserve
the terminology used by the user, organization, source material, or product.

Examples include `legacy`, `framework`, `workflow`, `pipeline`, `runtime`, `deploy`, `rollback`, `QA`,
`SDLC`, `API`, `SDK`, `CLI`, `frontend`, `backend`, `prompt`, `token`, `embedding`, `fine-tuning`,
`RAG`, `LLM`, `human-in-the-loop`, and `human-on-the-loop`. Also preserve terms such as `skill`,
`agent`, and `tool` when they name specific technical concepts.

These examples are illustrative, not exhaustive. Apply the same rule to similar terms according to
the domain and audience. When uncertain, keep the English term and use it consistently.
